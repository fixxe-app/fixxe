Rates are for reference, not for trading

FIXXE shows mid-market rates — the midpoint between global buy and sell prices. They are published by third-party providers and typically update once a day. They are not real-time trading quotes, and they are not the rate your bank, card issuer or exchange office will give you: those add their own spread and fees.
