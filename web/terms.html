Rates are for reference, not for trading

FIXXE shows mid-market rates — the midpoint between global buy and sell prices, published by third-party providers. The main sources update about once a minute; the reserve sources, used when the main ones are unavailable, update about once a day. Either way they are not real-time trading quotes, and they are not the rate your bank, card issuer or exchange office will give you: those add their own spread and fees. Cryptocurrency rates in particular can move sharply within minutes.

Do not rely on FIXXE as the sole basis for a financial decision, a payment, a trade, a contract, an invoice or a tax filing. Always check the actual rate with the party you are transacting with.

Your own rate adjustment

You can apply your own percentage to the rates — for example, to approximate your bank’s or exchange office’s margin. Amounts calculated this way are marked in the app as adjusted. They are your own calculation based on a figure you entered, not a rate offered by anyone. Unless you choose to keep it, the adjustment is cleared automatically after four hours without use.

By default the app picks the source automatically and falls back to the next one when a source is unavailable; you can also choose a source yourself.
